# Favicon SAS

Dibuat dengan tool imagegen bawaan, berdasarkan logo SAS yang diberikan pengguna.

Prompt: Edit the attached SAS logo into a production website favicon master for Sinar Asih Sumedang, a gadget shop. Preserve the exact letters SAS and the recognizable bold connected geometric monogram from reference. Produce a single square 1024x1024 flat icon, full bleed charcoal #373737 background with subtly rounded square corners. Enlarge centered SAS mark to occupy 86% of width and 58% height so readable at 32px. Bright cyan-teal #67d2e8 mark to match website primary teal #068da9 and excellent contrast, no gradients, no shadow, no textures, no white outer margin, no surrounding mockup, no extra words, no decorations. Precisely three readable letters S A S.

Master: `public/brand/sas-favicon-master.png`.
Browser: `src/app/favicon.ico` (16, 32, 48 px) dan `src/app/icon.png` (256 px).
